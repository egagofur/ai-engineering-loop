## google/elephant-goldfish-model (Elephant-Goldfish Model, EGM)

AEL ships a modified derivative of EGM as the opt-in `spec` host pack (`sync-hosts --pack spec`, issue #96).

- Upstream: https://github.com/google/elephant-goldfish-model (Copyright Google LLC; not an officially supported Google product)
- Fork vendored: https://github.com/egagofur/elephant-goldfish-model at the commit in `vendor/egm/UPSTREAM.json`
- License: Apache License 2.0, shipped as `adapters/spec/LICENSE`; modifications listed in `adapters/spec/NOTICE-AEL.md`
- Modifications: skills and personas renamed `egm-*` to `ael-spec-*` (`egm-drift-report` to `ael-spec-drift`), plugin-root and harness-specific references replaced with `ai-engineering-loop docs` lookups and AEL host rules, and a disclosed same-session fallback (`CONTEXT_ISOLATION_ONLY`) where a host has no subagent tool. `egm-bootstrap`, plugin manifests, tests and icons are not shipped.
- AEL files shipped: `adapters/spec/skills/ael-spec-{brainstorm,draft,review,review-loop,resolve,drift}/SKILL.md`, `adapters/spec/skills/ael-spec-draft/references/design_doc.md`, `adapters/spec/agents/ael-spec-*.md` (8 reviewer personas), `adapters/spec/standards/ael-spec-*.md` (5 document standards and the execution instructions), `adapters/spec/LICENSE` and `adapters/spec/NOTICE-AEL.md`. They are generated by `scripts/sync-spec-pack.js`; every file names the source commit and the skills keep `license: Apache-2.0`.
- Original AEL text (not derived from EGM): `core/spec-pack.md`, `lib/design-ref.js` (`goal draft --from-design`), design drift in `lib/impact-map.js`, and the spec status in `hosts doctor`.

## Adopted skill ideas (issue #36)

The following AEL files were written fresh for AEL. No upstream text was copied. They adapt ideas from the projects below, which are credited here.

### obra/superpowers

- Source: https://github.com/obra/superpowers
- License: MIT
- Ideas adapted: receiving-code-review (verify feedback before acting, decline with evidence), verification-before-completion (no completion claim without fresh evidence), and systematic-debugging (trace backward, question the design after three failed fixes, bisect test pollution).
- AEL files: `adapters/quality/skills/ael-receiving-code-review/SKILL.md` (prefixed `ael-` to avoid clashing with the dot-plugin skill of the same idea), the "No claim without fresh evidence" section of `references/loop-verify.md`, and "When fixes keep failing" in `core/root-cause-analysis.md`.

### worldflowai/everything-claude-code

- Source: https://github.com/worldflowai/everything-claude-code
- License: MIT (as stated in the project README)
- Ideas adapted: strategic-compact (compact at stage boundaries) and verification-loop (build, typecheck, lint, test, security order).
- AEL files: the compaction notes in `references/loop-goal.md` / `loop-verify.md` and the default order in the generated `.ai-engineering-loop/verification.md`.

### Cynthia-cym/project-onboarding

- Source: https://github.com/Cynthia-cym/project-onboarding
- License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Cynthia-cym
- Ideas adapted: evidence-first repository onboarding from structure, history, and code patterns.
- AEL files: `adapters/project-onboarding/` (writes `.ai-engineering-loop/architecture.md` and `conventions.md`).

### ael-code-simplifier

`adapters/quality/skills/ael-code-simplifier/SKILL.md` is an original AEL text for a common "simplify the diff without changing behavior" practice.
